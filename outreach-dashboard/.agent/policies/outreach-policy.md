# Outreach Execution Policy

Policy version: 2026-07-30

- Process only ICP >= 70, identity-verified, non-customer, non-exclusive-market, non-cooldown companies not contacted on the current Shanghai day.
- Allow one first-touch action per company per Shanghai calendar day.
- Rank candidate sources as customer attachments, local database, then Google discovery.
- Rank executable channels as verified business/procurement email, official procurement or supplier form, LinkedIn, Facebook, then Instagram.
- Use lower-priority channels only when no higher-priority channel is safely executable.
- A verified email route must never terminate the company workflow on a pre-send verification, configuration, authentication, compose-availability, or bounded technical failure. If no send click/customer interaction occurred, immediately continue in the same customer execution through first-party-verified LinkedIn, Facebook, then Instagram routes. A `send_unconfirmed` result or any evidence of a send click/customer interaction permanently stops cross-channel fallback and locks the company against replay.
- A website/contact-form route that fails during inspection or preparation before any submit interaction must continue immediately to an official social URL extracted from that first-party page. The queue's one-company selection is not a reason to suppress this same-task fallback. `submitted_confirmed`, submit uncertainty, or evidence of an irreversible submit interaction must stop fallback and lock replay.
- Prior read-only or pre-send website technical failures must not block a later read-only first-party reinspection whose sole purpose is validating an advertised official social route. All final company dedupe, identity, and uncertain-interaction gates still apply before any outreach action.
- A channel-specific `website_contact_unreachable_skip` or historical website failure circuit may not suppress an already first-party-verified official social queue row for the same company when the website evidence contains no send, submit, or customer interaction. This exception never applies to `failed_open`, `send_unconfirmed`, a physical click, or the Shanghai-day company retry circuit.
- In-task fallback and cross-run retry are different. During one bounded customer execution, verified alternate channels may be tried before a terminal result is recorded. Once that execution records `failed_open`, retire the entire company from every later queue selection for the rest of the current Shanghai day, regardless of the route or fallback platform, record `same_day_retry_circuit_open`, close the automation-owned tab, and immediately advance to the next safe company. Do not reopen the company in a later batch or full rerun that day.
- An exact task that records `website_contact_ready` or `website_contact_unreachable_skip` is also terminal for that task on the current Shanghai day. Discovery may add a genuinely different verified channel for the company, but it must never re-enqueue the identical task and starve later customers.
- A code repair never clears that Shanghai-day `failed_open` company circuit. The repair applies only to untouched companies and future days; verifier-fix exceptions must not reopen the failed company through email, website, or social fallback.
- Count only `sent_confirmed` and `submitted_confirmed`. Drafts, opens, likes, follows, connection requests, failures, bounces, and unconfirmed sends never count.
- Email requires sender `Leo@flextailgear.com`, a verified public business/procurement recipient, Alibaba Mail send success, and matching Sent-folder evidence.
- Every first-touch Alibaba Mail email must include exactly the approved FLEXTAIL attachment set: `One Page_Flextail_Monthly_Product_Roadmap_Sorted_2026-07.pdf`, `2026 Catalog (HD).pdf`, and `Distributor Network.png`. Before Send, the executor must verify all three exact filenames are visibly uploaded and no upload is pending. A missing, additional, stale, or unverified attachment is a pre-send technical failure; perform no send and continue through the safe channel fallback.
- Every development email must end with the owner-approved signature exactly: `Sincerely`, `Best Regard`, `Leo Liu`, `Overseas Business Unit`, `Tel/whatsapp: +86 17321028184`, `Email: Leo@flextailgear.com`, `SHANGHAI FLEXTAIL TECHNOLOGY CO.,LTD.`, and `5th Floor, No. 68, Yincheng Middle Road, Pudong New Area, Shanghai (Times Finance Center)`. A missing or modified signature fails before Send.
- Personal-domain addresses remain rejected by default. A narrow exception is allowed only for an ICP-qualified `sales_agency` when the exact address is explicitly published as the principal/agency business contact on the agency's own HTTPS website, the evidence URL is same-host first-party, and both `official_supplier_email_verified` and `official_public_business_email` machine statuses are present. Search results, directories, guessed addresses, non-agency targets, and mismatched evidence hosts never qualify.
- If live first-party enrichment also discovers a contact form and changes the route status to `official_contact_form_verified`, the agency personal-domain exception remains eligible only when that same first-party verification record explicitly contains `public_business_email`; a form signal alone never qualifies.
- Alibaba Mail web UI in the dedicated Chrome/CDP 9224 session is the only permitted email-sending surface. Never open, inspect, automate, or send through Foxmail or any desktop/alternate mail client; a configured alternate provider must fail closed before any email action.
- If Alibaba Mail's searchable recipient control leaves the exact expected address in the input after the first Enter, the executor may issue one bounded second Enter to commit the autocomplete selection. This is allowed only for an exact expected-address match and never replaces the rendered recipient, subject, and body verification gate.
- Alibaba Mail preflight and sending must reuse the same automation-owned dedicated 9224 tab when available. A transient login shell is not proof of logout: allow the existing authenticated session a bounded restoration window and require a stable repeated login state before reading credentials or declaring `alibaba_webmail_login_required`. Never run more than one credential attempt per company execution.
- When Alibaba Mail contains Sent-detail, draft, or other auxiliary iframes, recipient/subject/body verification and filling must scope the editor to the active `[data-testid="compose-container"]`; never select a generic page-level iframe that can bind to historical mail content.
- Physical recipient fallback must resolve the input through that same active compose container. It must never fall back to a page-global combobox or the mailbox search bar.
- Website forms require visible submission confirmation. Social messages require persistent visible outgoing-message evidence.
- A social draft with no explicit Send control and no Enter/send click is a pre-send `failed_open`, never `send_unconfirmed`. Only evidence of an irreversible send attempt may create the uncertainty lock.
- Alibaba recipient-chip discovery must cover committed Ant Design overflow items as well as selection items, stay scoped to the active compose container and recipient row, and still require the exact intended email in the opened tooltip before Send.
- If Alibaba changes chip class names, a bounded geometric fallback may inspect only small visible elements on the active recipient row and must record nearby structure on failure. Geometry may locate a candidate chip but can never verify identity; only an exact intended-email tooltip match may authorize Send.
- A social control without visible Send text is eligible only when its own `type`, `data-testid`, `data-control-name`, name, id, or class provides an explicit `send`/`submit` identity near the verified composer. Confirmation after the click remains mandatory; generic nearby controls are forbidden.
- A Facebook `profile.php?id=<numeric page id>` URL may execute only when the exact URL was cross-verified from the company's first-party website and `officialSocialProfileVerified=true`. Generic profile URLs, missing/non-numeric ids, personal-profile identity signals, search/watch/group/event routes, and unverified redirects remain blocked. A target-validation failure must be recorded as pre-send `failed_open` so it cannot silently replay or starve the queue.
- A represented brand's official social page is evidence that an agency represents that brand, not proof that the page is the agency's own account. Never message, follow, or like that brand page under the agency customer identity unless the first-party source separately proves the exact agency-owned social URL.
- A company website link to a founder or employee LinkedIn `/in/` profile proves association only, not authorization to use that personal profile as the company's official outreach channel. Company multichannel fallback may use only an identity-verified LinkedIn organization URL such as `/company/`; named-person outreach requires a separate buyer-identity workflow.
- An authenticated Sales Navigator session in the dedicated 9224 browser may research named buyers, capture a complete email visibly exposed by LinkedIn, and execute supported LinkedIn engagement, connection, or messaging controls only after exact company/buyer identity verification. An exact email may also be accepted when the company first-party website publishes that same address and identity. Masked, partial, pattern-generated, inferred, third-party-enriched, or search-snippet addresses remain unverified. Store the profile URL, evidence URL, verification method, and Shanghai verification time.
- For a LinkedIn-confirmed qualified buyer, use the shortest safe email path: capture the exact complete LinkedIn email, or one exact first-party company/vendor address when LinkedIn does not expose one; run company/recipient same-day and historical locks; then send directly through authenticated Alibaba Mail and verify Sent. Do not require an unrelated pool refill, secondary social action, or manual review before this email path. This shortcut never bypasses identity, exact-address, recipient/body/subject/attachment, duplicate, delivery, or confirmation gates.
- A user-supplied filtered Sales Navigator people search may serve as the buyer-source evidence without separate website research when every acted row visibly exposes the exact buyer name, qualifying buyer/procurement/category title, and current company. Connect requests must be serialized and deduplicated by profile/name. Do not impose an application-level numeric cap; use the verified queue, 45-minute resumable checkpoint, and every visible LinkedIn limit, restriction, CAPTCHA, cooldown, duplicate, and confirmation signal as the real stopping conditions. Connect remains non-counting engagement rather than `sent_confirmed` customer development.
- A personal LinkedIn `/in/` URL may enter the named-buyer Navigator workflow only when the company’s own HTTPS website links that exact profile, explicitly names the buyer, and supplies a same-host evidence URL. The executor must validate the buyer name on-page and record it as a named-buyer action; this never converts the personal profile into a company-owned social channel.
- Every post-email social attempt must persist an explicit secondary-channel status and evidence in the ledger. A validator or transport result that lacks `sendStatus` must be normalized to pre-send `failed_open` with its reason/error; it must never disappear as `social_result_missing` or be promoted to confirmed.
- Website contact navigation must reject cookie and consent controls, including Accept/Allow/Reject and preference-management actions, even when their href or surrounding page matches a contact route. Only explicit business/contact controls may be clicked.
- After each email batch, scan the authenticated Inbox and server-designated Junk/Spam mailbox for a bounded window for delivery-status failures. A matched DSN must immediately downgrade the company to `bounced`, remove it from confirmed counts, and permanently lock that recipient/company from automatic resend; ordinary auto-replies are delivery evidence only and never count as buyer replies or opportunities. Human replies found in either mailbox must be recorded as reply/opportunity evidence without replaying the first touch.
- When the owner confirms that a previously disabled sender has been restored, persist an exact sender plus restoration timestamp checkpoint. Historical sender-disabled DSNs before that checkpoint remain attached to their original companies and never authorize resend, but they must not permanently disable future net-new email attempts. Any sender-disabled DSN at or after the checkpoint immediately pauses email again.
- A company whose earlier same-day website inspection stopped only because the sender was disabled may use its newly first-party-verified email after the owner restoration checkpoint, but only when that failure predates restoration and contains no send click or customer interaction. This exception never applies to bounces, confirmed sends, uncertain sends, preserved populated drafts, or post-restoration failures.
- A `bounced` result is a permanent company and exact-recipient execution lock in discovery, queue construction, final pre-send deduplication, checkpoint reconciliation, and every later full rerun. A later Sent-folder record for the same failed address cannot promote it back to confirmed or authorize another delivery attempt.
- The daily maximum is 100 distinct confirmed companies. The default bounded run selects up to 25 companies, may be explicitly raised to at most 50, and always stops at 45 minutes.
- Missing, stale, or contradictory evidence must fail closed.
- Discovery and execution must share one `executionReadiness` decision. A URL alone is not an executable channel: website routes require first-party-verified supplier/form capability, email requires an official public business recipient, and social requires a first-party-cross-verified exact profile. Evidence-incomplete rows belong in the enrichment backlog and must not open Chrome or inflate executable-company counts.
- Email is a preferred route, never a batch-wide dependency. Authentication or pre-send failure affects only that email route: continue through the same company's verified website/social routes, then immediately advance to other safe companies and continue first-party enrichment. Never stop the batch merely because email is unavailable.
- Every production optimization or incident repair must be made durable in executable code, a regression test, this policy when the contract changes, and the production Prompt. A commentary-only workaround or one-run manual instruction is incomplete.
- Generated discovery, verification-state, Runtime, execution, ledger, and Dashboard artifacts must use bounded retry for transient Windows `UNKNOWN`/busy/permission file locks and atomic replacement where supported. A recoverable lock must not discard completed first-party research or require manual reruns.
- Potential-pool, daily-cap, and reserve capacity are measured in distinct companies, never channel rows. Multiple verified routes for one company remain fallback metadata and must not consume multiple company slots or truncate later evidence-ready companies.
- When distinct-company capacity falls below the 100-company target, replenish it with net-new ICP-qualified companies supported by current first-party website evidence. Capacity replenishment must never clear confirmed/unconfirmed company locks, recycle historical companies, invent channels, or weaken identity, compliance, duplicate, market, cooldown, or confirmation gates.
- Browser UI actions may target only the attached dedicated Chrome session on CDP 9224 by its exact CDP target ID and independent user-data-dir. Never enumerate, focus, activate, inspect, attach to, or reuse an operator Chrome window by OS process, window title, 9222, or ambient tabs; if the attached 9224 session is unavailable, record a technical failure and perform no browser action.
### Verified email execution precedence

- When first-party enrichment marks a public business email verified, the explicit email platform and verified recipient override any legacy `website-contact` task id or `email_priority` action label. Execute it through the Alibaba Mail recipient/subject/body/Sent confirmation gates.
- A watchdog timeout before any irreversible action locks the company for the current Shanghai day, but does not permanently retire the verified route on later days.
