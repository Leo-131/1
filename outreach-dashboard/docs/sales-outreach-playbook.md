# FLEXTAIL 全球客户开发话术与素材规范

## 目标

首触达不是立即成交，而是获得回复、正确负责人或 15 分钟产品范围会议。所有陈述必须有客户资料或官方页面证据，不虚构销量、合作关系、独家权利或买家身份。

## 渠道策略

- LinkedIn：先关注，再发 55–75 词专业 DM；不评论、不批量点赞。
- Facebook / Instagram：只联系已验证官方企业主页。DM 保持 55–85 词，首条不附图片、不堆链接。
- Email / 官网表单：80–130 词，可附一个最相关的 FLEXTAIL 官方产品集合链接。
- 未确认发送、验证码、无输入框或身份不一致：停止并记录，不重复点击发送。

## 客户分层角度

- 大型零售商 / Category Buyer：品类匹配、价格带覆盖、新品管线、vendor review。
- 经销商 / 进口商：区域覆盖、渠道匹配、可持续动销、分销评估负责人。
- 独立户外店：紧凑、易演示、适合露营徒步、可小范围试销。
- ODM / Sourcing：共同开发、使用场景、产品路线、采购负责人。

## 三阶段英文话术

### 首触达

Hi {company} team, nice to e-meet you. I’m Leo from FLEXTAIL, focused on ultralight outdoor and travel electrics. Your {category} assortment looks relevant to our compact pumps, lighting and portable-power range. We’re planning 36+ new SKUs for 2026 across several use cases and price tiers. Could you point me to the category buyer or vendor-review owner for a brief 15-minute introduction?

### 第一次跟进（3–5 天）

Hi {company} team — following up on my FLEXTAIL note. Our ultralight pumps, lighting and travel electrics cover multiple use cases and price tiers, which may fit your {category} assortment. Would a 15-minute range review be useful, or could you point me to the category buyer?

### 礼貌收口（7–10 天）

Hi {company} team — one last quick note from Leo at FLEXTAIL. If {category} is not a current priority, no problem. If it is, who owns the category or vendor review so I can send only the most relevant range?
