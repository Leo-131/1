# Direct provider input and bounded-by-default execution

The observed local preflight is already fast. Avoid another speculative scheduler
rewrite: reduce manual organization-envelope conversion and routine output first.
Accept shallow provider `organizations`, mapping only declared name/domain/site/id
and preserving explicit group aliases. This never creates ICP, contact evidence or
send eligibility. Existing research caches and all four permanent histories are
still read fresh on every invocation.

Default CLI output is a ten-row research packet after the full 100-row preflight.
`--packet-size N` overrides the packet; `--compact` explicitly exports the entire
pending audit list. Record each failed check once, regenerate after research or
receipts, and immediately send already-ready recipients without waiting for fill.

Browser executor uses IAB. Prefer compact visible DOM evidence and primary page
reads over unnecessary third-party WebMCP tool catalogs. A noisy research tab
should be closed after evidence capture; do not reset the authenticated mailbox.
This guidance does not claim to disable third-party tool notifications in code.

Do not recreate prior efficiency releases or rebuild/deploy for each research row.
Write every confirmed send immediately to permanent local history; cloud-sync the
completed verified increment as a group, before handoff, preserving actual dates.
Unknown outcomes require inspection, never retry. Safety gates and serial sending
are unchanged. Measure output bytes, not an unmeasured outreach time reduction.

Tests cover organization mapping, group locks, immutable input, malformed rows,
unchanged safety, full preflight counts and bounded default CLI output. Publish
only after tests/build pass, retaining owner-private access.
